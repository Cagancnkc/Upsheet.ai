Our Story

A Shopify merchant friend couldn't understand why his store's sales were dropping. Which products were actually selling? Which customer segment preferred which products? Were there seasonal trends? There was no data to answer any of these questions — only intuition.

To solve this, we built Mocksheets. It analyzes Shopify sales, customer behavior, and product performance — our trained AI model suggests concrete, data-driven actions like "which 5 products should you optimize to grow sales 25% in the next 3 months?"

From small stores to enterprise e-commerce teams, everyone can now make decisions backed by customer data. Not guesses — data. Not feelings — action.
