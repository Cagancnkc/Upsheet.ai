Frequently asked questions

Can I start without a credit card?

Yes. The Free plan is fully free and unlimited in time. Start using it right away — no credit card required.

How do I connect my Shopify store?

After upgrading to Pro or Ultra, connect your Shopify store in one click from the settings screen. OAuth handles the secure connection — you never share your password.

What's the difference with the annual plan?

With annual billing, Pro is $7/mo and Ultra is $19/mo — 20% cheaper than monthly. Billed once per year. Cancel any time.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored encrypted on Supabase infrastructure in the EU region. Fully GDPR-compliant — your data belongs to you and can be exported or deleted at any time.

How is Ultra different from Pro?

Pro gives you unlimited catalog size and standard AI speed. Ultra adds priority AI processing speed, early access to new features, and priority live support.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel any time from your account settings. Cancellation stops the next renewal; you keep access through the end of your current period.
