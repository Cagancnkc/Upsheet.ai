Mocksheets vs Catalog Health Pro — Which Is the Right Choice?

Comparison

Feature-by-feature comparison

Catalog Health Pro is a detection-focused tool that scans your catalog and lists issues with a clear health score. It's useful for quickly spotting common problems like missing SEO titles, short descriptions and missing images. It offers scan history logging.

Mocksheets offers similar detection capabilities — but sees detection as only a starting point. For the issues detected, it generates AI-powered fix suggestions; once you approve, it writes them directly to Shopify. Broader catalog operations are also in scope.

Decision Guide

When to pick which?

Choose Catalog Health Pro if: You only want a fast and clear answer to "what's missing in my catalog?", Catalog Health Pro's focused approach may be enough. If you prefer to fix the issues you detect yourself, or you are looking for a tool that fits your existing workflows, it's a sensible choice.

Choose Mocksheets if: You want the AI to fix the issues you detect and send them directly to Shopify after approval — Mocksheets adds this step. If you need broader catalog operations (vendor standardization, multilingual content), Mocksheets is a more comprehensive solution.

Decision Summary

  • Just "what's missing?" → Either one
  • AI fix + Shopify write-back → Mocksheets
  • Broader ops priority → Mocksheets
  • Fast, focused detection → Catalog Health Pro
FAQ

Frequently asked

Does Catalog Health Pro have a feature to fix the issues it detects?
Based on general information, Catalog Health Pro's focus is detection — whether it fixes automatically is unclear. We recommend checking the Shopify App Store page for the current feature list.
Are the health scores calculated with the same logic?
Each app has its own methodology — so the same catalog's score can come out different in two tools, which is normal. What matters is tracking the score's change over time within one tool.
