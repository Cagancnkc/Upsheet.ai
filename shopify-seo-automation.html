Problem

SEO apps show the problem, but don't solve it

Most Shopify SEO apps tell you "this product's title is missing" — but you still have to fix it manually. In a 500-product catalog, that can take weeks: open a screen for each product, write a title, come up with a meta description, and repeat.

Here's the Mocksheets difference: on top of detecting gaps, it generates suggestions in bulk with AI. You see suggestions for the entire catalog in a single table, tweak the ones you don't like, and push everything back to Shopify with one approval.

The result: weeks of one-by-one editing turn into minutes of bulk approval.

The Cost of Doing It by Hand

  • Average per product: 2-3 minutes (title + meta + tag)
  • 500 products = ~20 hours of uninterrupted work
  • Inconsistent output (different tone for the same product family)
  • Every new product goes through the same process

Bigger team means bigger problem; smaller team means falling behind.

FAQ

Frequently asked

How is this different from SEO apps like Smart SEO or Yoast?
Most apps give you one-by-one editing UIs and are strong on technical SEO areas like schema markup and sitemaps. Mocksheets focuses on bulk AI editing — analyzing hundreds of products at once and generating suggestions. Two different approaches for different needs: apps for technical SEO infrastructure, Mocksheets for content production.
Will it break my existing Shopify SEO settings?
No. Only changes you approve are written back to Shopify; nothing you don't approve changes. Every suggestion comes with "accept / skip / edit" options.
Does it cover image SEO (alt text) too?
Yes. Catalog Check also detects missing alt text. AI suggests descriptive alt text based on the product title and category; you push it back to Shopify with bulk approval.
