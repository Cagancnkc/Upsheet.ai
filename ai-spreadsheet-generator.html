Frequently Asked Questions about AI Spreadsheet Generation

Can AI generate a spreadsheet for me?

Yes. Mocksheets uses Claude AI to generate complete spreadsheets from plain English descriptions. Simply describe what you need (e.g., "Create a monthly budget tracker with income, expenses by category, and a savings summary") and Mocksheets generates a complete, ready-to-use spreadsheet.

What types of spreadsheets can the AI generator create?

The generator can create any data table: budget trackers, inventory lists, project plans, employee rosters, sales reports, customer databases, product catalogs, survey results, financial models, and any custom tabular structure you describe.

Does the AI spreadsheet generator support formulas?

Yes. When you describe calculated columns (e.g., "add a total column that multiplies quantity by unit price"), Mocksheets includes the appropriate formulas in the spreadsheet automatically.

Is it compatible with Google Sheets?

Yes. Mocksheets exports as .xlsx (Excel format) and .csv — both fully compatible with Google Sheets. Simply upload the downloaded file to Google Drive.

Do I need Excel knowledge to use this?

No. Mocksheets is designed for users without spreadsheet expertise. Just describe what you want in plain English and the AI handles all the technical work for you.
