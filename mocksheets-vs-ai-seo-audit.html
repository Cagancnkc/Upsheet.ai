Comparison

Feature-by-feature comparison

AI SEO Audit runs inside the Shopify admin (embedded) — you can use it directly from the admin panel without opening another tab. It scans your catalog against roughly 50 checkpoints: products, collections, pages and blog posts included. It offers both manual approval and full auto-application through "Automate" mode; it has an established, active user base.

Mocksheets runs as a separate web application — which may mean an extra open tab outside the Shopify admin. In return it covers broader catalog operations such as vendor/category standardization and multilingual content generation. Every change is presented for your approval before being written to Shopify.

Decision Guide

When to pick which?

Choose AI SEO Audit if: Working without ever leaving the Shopify admin, using the fully automatic mode and being comfortable with an English interface are priorities for you — AI SEO Audit is a strong choice with an established user base and broader page scanning (blog, collection included).

Choose Mocksheets if: You need broader catalog operations beyond SEO — such as vendor/category standardization, multilingual content generation and holistic management of catalog health — Mocksheets fills this gap.

Honesty note: Mocksheets doesn't currently run inside the Shopify admin (embedded) — it's a separate web application. For some users, this may mean opening an extra tab.

Decision Summary

  • Embedded + auto mode → AI SEO Audit
  • Broad scan including blog/collection → AI SEO Audit
  • Broader ops priority → Mocksheets
  • Vendor/category ops + multilingual → Mocksheets
  • You want to approve every change → Mocksheets
FAQ

Frequently asked

Does Mocksheets have a fully automated option like AI SEO Audit's "Automate" mode?
Not right now — Mocksheets asks for your approval before writing any change to Shopify. This is designed with a safety-first rather than speed-first mindset: no change is written to the live store without approval.
Can I use both together?
Technically yes, but if the same meta fields (meta title, description, alt text) are managed by two apps there is a risk of conflict — one may overwrite what the other wrote. We recommend not managing the same field with two apps.
How much is the price difference?
For current prices, check Mocksheets' pricing page and AI SEO Audit's Shopify App Store listing — both can change over time.
