Problem

Empty or copy-paste descriptions hurt sales

Supplier product data usually has either no description at all or the same generic text across every product. This hurts SEO (Google won't rank duplicate content) and conversions (customers can't answer "why is this product different?").

Hiring a copywriter can cost $5-10 per product — thousands of dollars for a large catalog. Freelance teams take weeks and brand consistency slips.

Mocksheets answers both at once: in seconds, a unique description for every product, all written back to Shopify in a single approval step.

The Cost of the Description Gap

  • Low ranking in Google search results
  • High bounce rate on the product page
  • Add-to-cart rate 20-30% lower
  • Lost conversions from social media traffic

A product with no description is a half-invisible product.

Solution

Bulk description generation in 3 steps

Select Products

FAQ

Frequently asked

Will all the descriptions look the same?
No. Each product is written uniquely based on its own title, category, and attributes. AI approaches each product with a different emphasis even within the same product family — no copy-paste output.
Can I preserve my brand voice?
Right now a general "professional + warm" tone is used; brand-specific voice tuning is on our roadmap. Today you can quickly edit suggestions at the approval step to match your brand voice.
Can I generate multilingual descriptions?
Yes. The "Translate Products" option in AI Actions lets you generate the same description in English, German, Arabic, and more. Practical for stores selling internationally.
