Decision Guide

Which tool is right for you?

Choose Plug in SEO if: You want comprehensive technical SEO auditing and recommendations. You have the bandwidth to implement fixes manually. You care deeply about schema markup and Google Search Console integration.

Choose Mocksheets if: You want to fix SEO issues fast, in bulk, without manual work. You want AI-generated content, not just recommendations. You want action, not just a to-do list.

Both together: Let Plug in SEO identify all technical SEO gaps. Use Mocksheets to auto-generate and bulk-implement fixes. Audit + AI action = comprehensive SEO strategy.

FAQ

Questions answered

Does Plug in SEO generate product descriptions?
Plug in SEO audits your product data against predefined SEO rules (e.g., title length, description presence). It flags issues but doesn't generate content. Mocksheets generates descriptions, titles, and bulk edits using AI analysis of visitor behavior.
What's the difference between rule-based and AI-driven SEO?
Rule-based (Plug in SEO): Checks if title is 50-60 chars, description exists, etc. AI-driven (Mocksheets): Analyzes which products visitors view but don't buy, then generates descriptions tailored to fix that gap. AI is more proactive.
Can I use both together?
Yes. Let Plug in SEO audit your technical SEO compliance. Use Mocksheets to generate content that fixes the issues. Audit + Action.
Which tool will improve my conversion rate?
Plug in SEO improves discoverability (SEO rankings). Mocksheets improves conversion (better product data for buyers already on your site). Both help sales, but in different ways.
