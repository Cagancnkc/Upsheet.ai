Decision Guide

Which tool is right for you?

Choose Hypotenuse AI if: You need fast, simple bulk description generation. You have product data but just need copy written quickly. You don't have Shopify analytics connected or don't want to analyze visitor behavior.

Choose Mocksheets if: You want to act on real data: which products underperform and why. You want descriptions generated specifically to fix conversion gaps. You want a full catalog health audit, not just copy generation.

The difference: Hypotenuse: "Generate descriptions for all products." Mocksheets: "Generate descriptions for products with high views but low conversion, tailored to fix that gap."

FAQ

Questions answered

Is Hypotenuse AI the same as Mocksheets?
No. Hypotenuse generates descriptions from product data you provide. Mocksheets analyzes your Shopify analytics and visitor behavior, then generates descriptions based on real gaps in your sales funnel. Different inputs = different outputs.
Can Hypotenuse analyze visitor behavior?
No. Hypotenuse reads your product data (SKU, category, specs) and generates copy. Mocksheets reads Shopify analytics (bounce rates, conversion gaps, popular products) and generates copy tailored to your actual visitor behavior.
Which generates better product descriptions?
Depends on your needs. Hypotenuse is fast for bulk basic descriptions. Mocksheets is more targeted: it identifies which products need help most and generates descriptions that fix why they underperform. Quality vs speed trade-off.
Can I use both?
Yes. Hypotenuse can generate quick descriptions. Mocksheets can optimize them further based on visitor behavior. But most stores find one is enough.
