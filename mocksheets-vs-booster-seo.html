Mocksheets vs Booster SEO — Which Should You Choose?

Booster SEO is a mature, well-rounded Shopify SEO app that covers image compression, schema markup, and meta automation through templates. Mocksheets approaches SEO from a different angle — AI content generation and natural language catalog commands, without predefined templates.

Comparison

Feature-by-feature comparison

Booster SEO & Image Optimizer is one of Shopify's most popular SEO apps. It offers a comprehensive set of technical and on-page SEO features: automatic alt text generation through templates, meta title/description formatting rules, JSON-LD structured data, Google Search Console integration, and image compression to improve page speed. It is designed around rule-based automation — you define a pattern, it applies it at scale. It has a large user base, positive reviews, and years of development behind it.

Mocksheets takes a fundamentally different approach. Instead of defining templates and rules, you describe what you want in plain language — "generate unique, SEO-optimized descriptions for all products in the Footwear collection" — and the AI writes content with full context from your store. It also provides a catalog health score (0–100) that surfaces your biggest content gaps automatically, something template-based tools can't do.

Decision Guide

When to pick which?

Choose Booster SEO if: Your primary SEO goals are technical — structured data markup, page speed improvement through image compression, Google Search Console integration, and automatically formatted meta titles using templates. Booster SEO is a strong, established choice for covering the technical foundation of Shopify SEO.

Choose Mocksheets if: You need to generate original, unique content — descriptions, SEO titles, and meta text that aren't just formatted templates but actually written by AI with knowledge of your products. Also choose Mocksheets for catalog health scoring and natural language bulk operations that go beyond what rule-based tools support.

Both together: These tools are highly complementary. Booster SEO handles the technical layer (schema, images, speed); Mocksheets handles the content layer (AI-generated text, catalog intelligence). Running both on the same store covers more ground than either alone.

Decision Summary

  • Schema markup / structured data → Booster SEO
  • Image compression → Booster SEO
  • AI content generation → Mocksheets
  • Catalog health scoring → Mocksheets
  • GSC integration → Booster SEO
  • Natural language bulk edits → Mocksheets

Because they cover different SEO layers, using both is a valid and common choice for growing Shopify stores.

FAQ

Frequently asked

Does Mocksheets compress images like Booster SEO?
No. Mocksheets focuses on AI content generation and catalog management — product descriptions, SEO titles, meta text, and catalog health scoring. Image compression and Core Web Vitals are Booster SEO's strengths. If image performance is your primary concern, Booster SEO is the right tool for that need.
Can I use both Mocksheets and Booster SEO together?
Yes, and many merchants do. Booster SEO handles technical SEO — image compression, schema markup, Google Search Console. Mocksheets handles content intelligence — AI-generated descriptions and titles, catalog health scoring, and natural language bulk operations. They cover different layers and don't duplicate each other.
Booster SEO has templates for alt text — does Mocksheets?
Mocksheets generates alt text using AI that understands the product's context — title, description, tags, and collection. Rather than a pattern like "Product Name - Brand - Store Name", it writes contextually relevant alt text for each product. Both approaches fill the same gap, but with different levels of specificity and uniqueness.
Which tool is better for technical SEO?
Booster SEO is stronger for technical SEO — structured data, schema markup, Google Search Console integration, and site speed. Mocksheets is stronger for content-level SEO — generating optimized descriptions, titles, and meta text using AI with full catalog context.
