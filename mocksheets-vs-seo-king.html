Mocksheets vs SEO King — Which Should You Choose?

SEO King lets you bulk-edit SEO fields with templates and find/replace rules — a powerful approach for stores that know exactly which patterns to apply. Mocksheets takes a different path: you write a command in plain language, and the AI handles the logic, generating new content rather than reformatting existing data.

Comparison

Feature-by-feature comparison

SEO King is an established Shopify app for bulk SEO editing. It lets you define templates with variables like [product_title], [vendor], and [collection] to generate formatted meta titles and descriptions across your catalog. It also supports find/replace operations so you can update text patterns across thousands of products at once. It's particularly effective for stores that want predictable, rule-based control over their SEO field formatting.

Mocksheets approaches catalog SEO from the other direction. Instead of defining rules and templates, you describe the outcome you want: "write unique, keyword-rich descriptions for all products in the Bags collection that currently have fewer than 100 words". The AI reads your catalog, understands each product's context, and generates original content — not reformatted templates. It also produces a catalog health score (0–100) to help you prioritize where to start.

Decision Guide

When to pick which?

Choose SEO King if: You have a clear, consistent SEO format you want to apply — for example, "Title | Brand | Store Name" as the meta title structure for every product. SEO King's template syntax gives you precise, predictable control over field formatting. It's also the stronger choice for find/replace operations across large numbers of products where you know exactly what to swap.

Choose Mocksheets if: You need AI to generate content that doesn't currently exist — unique product descriptions, SEO titles that reflect actual product benefits rather than a formula, or meta text optimized for search intent. Mocksheets also handles catalog health analysis: identifying which products have the worst SEO gaps so you can prioritize intelligently.

Both together: Template-based editing and AI generation serve different tasks. SEO King can handle consistent formatting; Mocksheets can fill in genuine content gaps. Used together, they cover the full spectrum from structural formatting to original content creation.

Decision Summary

  • Consistent template formatting → SEO King
  • AI content generation → Mocksheets
  • Find/replace at scale → SEO King
  • Catalog health scoring → Mocksheets
  • No template knowledge needed → Mocksheets
  • Predictable rule-based control → SEO King

Both tools address SEO at scale, but from opposite directions: SEO King structures existing data, Mocksheets generates new content.

FAQ

Frequently asked

Can Mocksheets do the same find/replace operations as SEO King?
Mocksheets supports natural language instructions like "replace all mentions of discontinued-brand with new-brand in product titles" — the AI interprets the intent and applies it. However, SEO King offers a more structured find/replace interface that some users find more predictable for strictly rule-based text swaps across thousands of products.
Does SEO King use AI to write content?
SEO King focuses on bulk editing existing fields using templates and find/replace logic. It reorganizes and reformats existing data. It does not generate new content from scratch. Mocksheets generates entirely new descriptions, titles, and meta text using AI with full knowledge of your product catalog.
Which tool is easier to learn?
Mocksheets has a lower learning curve for most users. You type what you want in plain language — "optimize all product titles in the Accessories collection for SEO" — and the AI handles the logic. SEO King requires understanding its template variable syntax and rule structure, which takes more initial configuration time.
Can I use both together?
Yes. SEO King's template-based approach works well for predictable, rule-driven edits across large datasets. Mocksheets is better for AI-generated new content and catalog health analysis. Using both is viable if your workflow has both types of tasks — structural formatting and original content creation.
