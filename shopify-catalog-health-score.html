Problem

Scattered tools, scattered visibility

Every Shopify store runs multiple apps — one for SEO, one for images, one for inventory. There's no single place to answer the general "how's my catalog doing?" question.

The result: once a week you have to open four different tabs and dig through reports. It's hard to tell which is priority; while improving one category, you don't notice another is breaking.

Catalog Health Score changes that: one number tells you the overall state, and the breakdown underneath shows which category is dragging it down. Click a low category and you jump straight to the fix screen.

What the Score Measures

  • SEO: title, meta, alt text completeness
  • Description: length, duplicate check
  • Image: every product has at least 1 image
  • Tags: categorization and filter fit

Each category 0-100, overall score is a weighted average.

FAQ

Frequently asked

How is the score calculated?
Each product's SEO field completeness, description length (min. 20 characters), presence of at least one image, and category tag status are checked. A product percentage is calculated per category, then a weighted average is taken to produce an overall 0-100 score.
Can I see score history?
Yes. The Dashboard trend chart draws change over 7, 30, and 90 days. After a bulk fix, you can visually track how your score jumped.
Is Google Shopping compliance included in the score?
Not currently — core catalog quality (SEO, description, image, tags) is included. A separate compliance score for Google Shopping-specific fields (GTIN, brand, condition) is on our roadmap.
