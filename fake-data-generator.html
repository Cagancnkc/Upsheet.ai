Frequently Asked Questions about Fake Data Generation

What is a fake data generator?

A fake data generator creates realistic but entirely fictional data for use in software development, testing, and demos. Unlike real data, fake data contains no actual personal information, making it safe to use in non-production environments.

Is fake data from Mocksheets GDPR compliant?

Yes. All data is completely synthetic — no real names, emails, or personal information is used or referenced. The data is safe for development, QA testing, demos, and training environments under GDPR.

What types of fake data can Mocksheets generate?

Mocksheets generates fake names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, dates, numeric values, UUIDs, company names, product data, transaction records, and any custom data type you describe in plain English.

Can I generate fake customer data for demos?

Yes. Ask Mocksheets to "generate 200 fake customer records with name, email, phone, address, and account creation date" and it will create a complete, realistic fake customer dataset ready to download as CSV or Excel.

How is fake data different from mock data?

The terms are often used interchangeably. "Fake data" typically refers to data that looks like real personal information but is entirely fictional. "Mock data" refers to data that mimics a system's real data structure. Mocksheets handles both use cases.
